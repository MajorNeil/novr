## Eye tracking (optional)
- Uses the headset's eye tracking through OpenXR (`XR_EXT_eye_gaze_interaction`). Without an eye tracker, or if the runtime doesn't offer it, everything stays on the mouse and head as before.
- Look-and-click menus: the cursor follows your eyes and snaps to buttons; the game's Select button clicks.
- Look to designate targets, a gaze reticle in flight, and gaze contact info.
- Settings > Eye Tracking has the options.

## Threat awareness
- Peripheral threat ring with time to impact, notch marks and friendlies, a missile banner, and directional missile tones.
- Minimap zoom-out, and radar warning and jamming icons placed for VR.

## New VR menus
- A menu hangar: you sit at a desk beside a parked aircraft, with the menu on a briefing board and aircraft flying past outside. The door closes while a mission loads.
- Main menu, Settings, Single Player, Multiplayer, Workshop and Key Bindings rebuilt in the game's own style. They all work the same way with mouse, gaze, gamepad or stick.
- Menu Controls (main menu or Settings) sets dedicated menu bindings for keyboard, gamepad and joystick. Joysticks are read the way the game reads them, so HOTAS hats bind correctly.
- The joystick menu bindings also move the cursor through the game's own menus, such as the encyclopedia.
- Key Bindings: set, add, clear or invert any action per device. The game's own binding screen is still there for calibration and restoring defaults.

## Install
Close Nuclear Option, run `NOVR.Installer-Win.exe` (or `NOVR.Installer-Linux`), and click Install. It updates an existing NOVR install in place and keeps your settings.
